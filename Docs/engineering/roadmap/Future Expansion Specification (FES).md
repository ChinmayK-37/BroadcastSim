1. Purpose

The Future Expansion Specification (FES) defines the planned evolution of BroadcastSim beyond Version 1.

Its objectives are to:

Provide a long-term roadmap
Demonstrate architectural scalability
Ensure future features integrate without major redesign
Maintain backward compatibility

This document is informational and does not define implementation requirements for Version 1.
